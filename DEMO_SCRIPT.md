# Driftlog updated demo

Target length: 60-75 seconds.

## Before recording

1. Open the app once and wait for `23 MB semantic model · ready`.
2. Clear any private entries.
3. Record the app at 1080p with the browser zoom near 90-100%.
4. Keep the cursor away from the text while speaking.

## Recording route

### 0:00-0:07 — Start on the empty dark constellation

**Say:**

> This is Driftlog, a private offline journal that turns the people, places, topics, and feelings in your day into a constellation.

### 0:07-0:15 — Point to the two local-model indicators

Show `offline · local brain` and `23 MB semantic model · ready`.

**Say:**

> The new build ships with its own 23-megabyte semantic model. There is no model API, account, analytics, or journal-data network call.

### 0:15-0:31 — Paste the clean fixture

Paste:

> At the library, I listened to "A Better Way" and worked on #MemoryGrove. I felt drained, but proud of what I built.

Pause so the detected chips can be seen: `Library`, `Memorygrove`, `A Better Way`, `Proud`, and `Tired`.

**Say:**

> Recognition now understands bounded places, quoted works, explicit topics, and feeling families. It reads drained as tired without confusing words like satisfied with sad.

### 0:31-0:45 — Map the entry

Click **Map to stars**, pause, then hover one or two nodes.

**Say:**

> One click maps the entry. Related details connect, repeated details grow, and the original writing stays in local browser storage.

### 0:45-0:58 — Show the log and model fallback

Open **Log**, select the entry, return to **Write**, then toggle **semantic brain off** briefly.

**Say:**

> The model runs in a background worker, and it is optional. Pause it and Driftlog's deterministic recognizer still works instantly.

Turn the semantic brain back on.

### 0:58-1:10 — Finish on the constellation

Use **Fit to screen** and leave the constellation centered.

**Say:**

> After the first load, the app, runtime, and model are cached on the device, so Driftlog keeps working without internet. Your day stays yours.

## Optional offline proof shot

For a longer cut, load Driftlog once, wait for the model to become ready, disconnect the computer from the network, refresh, and map a second non-private fixture. Keep this as a short end card rather than interrupting the main story.
